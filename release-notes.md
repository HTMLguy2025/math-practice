# Version 3.0.0

## Simple Operations

- Tap a digit button to open that operation's menu. Add **negatives** or **decimals**, or turn on **Mix with regular** to shuffle those in and out at random — some questions plain, some with one, some with both.
- **Practice points** now show at the top right while you work. Decimal questions earn the most, negatives a little extra, and bigger numbers more than small ones.
- The **Finish** button glows once you've earned enough points to keep your streak going, and catches fire when you've reached a bonus.
- New **Auto-Complete** switch under the check button: leave it on and answers submit the moment they match, or turn it off to press the check button or Enter yourself. It remembers your choice.

## Mix & Match

Build your own set of simple operations and practice them shuffled together.

- **Build a Mix** on the home page and tick any of the operations and sizes you want.
- Your picks are remembered, so **Replay last mix** starts the same set again in one tap.
- One running score follows you through the whole mix.

## Streaks and scores

- Your **top score** only changes when a session is genuinely better — a higher accuracy, or the same accuracy over more questions.
- A streak you've let slip now correctly shows **No streak** instead of freezing at its last count.

## Look and feel

- A darker theme with rounded buttons, a subtle grid backdrop, and card layouts on the home page.
- The **Finish** button stays on screen at all times, tucked in the bottom-right corner.
- Menus open and exercises start without waiting.
- Simple-operation practice fits on screen without scrolling, the check mark is properly centred, and the division label reads correctly.
