# Version 4.0.0

## One daily point balance

- **Every activity now earns points.** Each correct answer is worth more on harder activities and less on easier ones, so counting is a little, nested fractions are a lot.
- There is **one streak** for everything you practice. Reach your daily goal on a weekday to keep it going. The goal grows a little as your streak does.
- **Weekends are optional.** Weekend practice is saved and can earn a bonus, but skipping it never breaks your streak.
- Points are saved the moment you answer, so nothing is lost if you leave without pressing Finish.
- The home page shows your streak and today's progress in one place, and every activity shows how many points you have today. The **Finish** button lights up when you have reached your goal, and catches fire when you earn a bonus.
- Mix & Match earns points too.

## My progress

- A new **My progress** page on the home page shows a calendar of the month. Days show your points, and a check mark for a reached goal or a star for a bonus.
- Tap any day to see how many answers were right and what you practiced.
- **Save a copy** of your progress to keep it safe, or **Load a copy** on another device.

## Top score

- Your top score now updates as soon as you beat it, instead of waiting until you press Finish.
