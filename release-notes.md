# Version 3.1.0

## Themes

- A new **settings gear** sits in the top corner of the home page. Tap it to change how the whole app looks.
- Choose from five themes: **Midnight**, **Forest**, **Ember**, **Amethyst** and **Rose**.
- Or pick **any color you like**. Buttons and highlights are tuned to that color automatically, and kept light-on-dark so they stay easy to read, even for very bright or very pale picks.
- Choose a **secondary color** too: it sets the second end of gradients and the thin trims around cards, panels and headers. Each theme comes with its own.
- Your theme is remembered and applies to every exercise, including Mix & Match.
