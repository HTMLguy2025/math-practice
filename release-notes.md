# Version 2.3.0

## Streak tracking

Each Simple Operations exercise (Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction, Division) now tracks a **daily weekday streak** independently.

- Earn points by answering correctly — harder questions are worth more
- Hit the daily point goal to extend your streak
- The goal gradually increases the longer your streak runs
- Missing a weekday resets your streak; long absences (2+ weeks) also reset it
- Streaks are shown in the launcher with an animated flame
- The flame label changes style as your streak grows longer
