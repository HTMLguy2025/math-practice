# Version 4.2.2

## Streaks

- **Your streak starts on the first day you reach your goal**, and reaching your goal on a Saturday or Sunday now counts toward it too.
- Skipping a weekend still never breaks your streak.

## Learn how, then practice

- **Every activity now has a short lesson.** Tap **? How to** at the top of any exercise, or the **?** beside an activity on the home page, to watch it worked out step by step. There are 43 lessons, from counting to nested fractions, and a **Tutorials** page on the home page lists them all.
- Lessons never rush you. Press **Next** when you are ready (it glows once the picture has finished), and finish with a few quick questions to try it yourself.
- Sums are shown the way you write them on paper: numbers stacked, carrying and borrowing, long multiplication, and long division. Carrying and borrowing are explained step by step, including where the carried number comes from.
- You can have a lesson read aloud, and slow it down. The lesson opens on top of your practice, so you never lose your place or your score.

## Help with the question in front of you

- **Not quite? Tap Show me how.** After a wrong answer, a worked example of that exact question is waiting for you.
- **Stuck? Tap Hint** at the bottom of the screen to see the working before you answer. A hint halves the points for that question.
- Exercises that might need some scratch paper say so.

## One daily point balance

- **Every activity now earns points.** Each correct answer is worth more on harder activities and less on easier ones, so counting is a little, nested fractions are a lot.
- There is **one streak** for everything you practice. Reach your daily goal on a weekday to keep it going. The goal grows a little as your streak does.
- **Weekends are optional.** Weekend practice is saved and can earn a bonus, but skipping it never breaks your streak.
- Points are saved the moment you answer, so nothing is lost if you leave without pressing Finish.
- The home page shows your streak and today's progress in one place, and every activity shows how many points you have today. The **Finish** button lights up when you have reached your goal, and catches fire when you earn a bonus.
- Mix & Match earns points too.

## My progress

- A new **My progress** page on the home page shows a calendar of the month, with Sunday first. Days show your points, and a check mark for a reached goal or a star for a bonus.
- Tap any day to see how many answers were right and what you practiced.
- **Save a copy** of your progress to keep it safe, or **Load a copy** on another device.

## Top score

- Your top score now updates as soon as you beat it, instead of waiting until you press Finish.
